Automatic Bi-Directional Gate Mechanism

An automated bi-directional gate mechanism designed as an intelligent access system that detects movement from both entering and exiting directions, processes the signal, and automatically drives a motor to open and close the gate.

The system follows an Approach → Pass → Close routine regardless of which side a vehicle approaches from, combining detection, processing, actuation, clearance, and closing.
